# GAME DESIGN

**Petualangan Menuju Cahaya** — original world, original characters, original music.

## World & story

Perjalanan melewati delapan wilayah: Desa Awal, Hutan Kabut, Lembah Batu, Pegunungan Awan, Danau Cahaya, Kota Tua, Hutan Gelap, Gerbang Cahaya. Delapan chapter story. Setelah 33 siluman: quest `Lampah Pungkasan` (bukan game over permanen).

NPC utama: Bahasa Jawa. UI: Bahasa Indonesia. Soal: kelas 1 SD, huruf besar, pilihan A/B/C.

## 33 Siluman

Roster original (Klowo Alas … RAKSA CAHAYA PETENG) dengan id, title, region, element, skills, weakness, lore, bossType. Nama story legacy (Jaladara/Adityara) tetap untuk kompatibilitas.

## Combat & transformation

Pukulan, tendangan, skill, dodge, block. Damage server-side.  
Wujud original: Asal → Cahaya → Kilat → Agung → Fajar (bukan salinan franchise).

## Quest & education

Main, side, daily, weekly, guild, education. State: LOCKED / AVAILABLE / ACTIVE / COMPLETED / CLAIMED. Reward tidak double-claim.

## Economy & crafting

Gold server-generated. Gather, craft, NPC shop. Tidak ada real-money, gacha berbayar, atau gambling.

## Social

Party, guild, chat + filter, report. Trade atomic.

## Instances

Dungeon, raid 10–20 (`Lampah Pungkasan`), world boss, world event `Bayangan Ing Alas`, season Festival Cahaya, leaderboard server-side.

## Endgame

Level 50+, difficulty NORMAL/HARD/NIGHTMARE, loop event/raid/guild/crafting/achievement.
